Comparison normalization: Unicode NFC; ignore punctuation, whitespace and verse numerals; normalize avagraha punctuation. Vowel lengths, consonants and pitch marks are not discarded. Alternatives printed inside a verse are retained and may produce a comparison difference. Karavalamba’s differing-length editions are compared positionally, not asserted to be one-to-one.
