Opening prayers

ಕನ್ನಡदेवनागरीEnglish (Roman)
Family reading of the five opening invocations, checked against recording 1. Not certified against a Sringeri edition. Sanskrit in Kannada, Devanagari and Roman scripts; these are not Vedic accented passages.

Opening prayers

01

śuklāmbaradharaṃ viṣṇuṃ śaśivarṇaṃ caturbhujam |
prasannavadanaṃ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye ||

02

gururbrahmā gururviṣṇurgururdevo maheśvaraḥ |
guruḥ sākṣāt paraṃ brahma tasmai śrīgurave namaḥ ||

03

yā kundendutuṣārahāradhavalā yā śubhravastrāvṛtā
yā vīṇāvaradaṇḍamaṇḍitakarā yā śvetapadmāsanā |
yā brahmācyutaśaṅkaraprabhṛtibhirdevaiḥ sadā pūjitā
sā māṃ pātu sarasvatī bhagavatī niḥśeṣajāḍyāpahā ||

04

mṛtyuñjayāya rudrāya nīlakaṇṭhāya śambhave |
amṛteśāya śarvāya mahādevāya te namaḥ ||

05

namaḥ sūryāya candrāya maṅgalāya budhāya ca |
guruśukraśanibhyaśca rāhave ketave namaḥ ||
