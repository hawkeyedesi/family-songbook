Short bhajans in Ajji’s opening sequence. Family reading checked against the recording; not a Sringeri-verified edition. Repetitions follow the audio.

Rama bhajans and mantra

01

raghupati rāghava rājā rāma |
patita pāvana sītā rāma ||
īśvara allāha tere nāma |
sabako sanmati de bhagavān ||

02

hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare |
hare kṛṣṇa hare kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa hare hare ||

03

rāmāya rāmabhadrāya rāmacandrāya vedhase |
raghunāthāya nāthāya sītāyāḥ pataye namaḥ ||
śrī rāma jaya rāma jaya jaya rāma ||
