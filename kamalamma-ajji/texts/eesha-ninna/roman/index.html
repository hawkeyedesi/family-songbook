Eesha Ninna Charana Bhajane

ಕನ್ನಡदेवनागरीEnglish (Roman)
Kannada composition shown in three scripts, not translated into Sanskrit. Published version; Ajji’s repetitions and small oral variants follow her recording.

Eesha Ninna Charana Bhajane

01

īśa ninna caraṇa bhajanè āśèyiṃda māḍuvènu
doṣarāśi nāśamāḍu śrīśa keśava, śrīśa keśava |

02

śaraṇu hòkkènayya ènna maraṇa samayadalli ninna
caraṇa smaraṇè karuṇisayya nārāyaṇa ||

03

śodhisènna bhavada kaluṣa bhodhisayya jñānavènagè
bādhisuva yamana bādhè biḍisu mādhava , biḍisu mādhava |

04

hiṃdaneka yonigaḻali baṃdu baṃdu nòṃdènayya
iṃdu bhavada baṃdha biḍiso taṃdè goviṃda ||

05

bhraṣṭanènisabeḍa kṛṣṇa iṣṭu mātra beḍikòṃbè
śiṣṭaròḍanè iṭṭu kaṣṭa biḍisu viṣṇuve , viṣṇuve |

06

madananayya ninna mahimè vadanadalli nuḍiyuvaṃtè
hṛdayadalli hudugisayya madhusūdana ||

07

kavidukòṃḍu iruva pāpa savèdu poguvaṃtè māḍi
javana bādhèyannu biḍiso śrītrivikrama, śrītrivikrama |

08

kāmajanaka ninna nāma premadiṃda pāḍuvaṃtha
nemavènagè pālisayya svāmi vāmana ||

09

mòdalu ninna pādapūjè òdaguvaṃtè māḍo ènna
hṛdayadòḻagè sadana māḍu mudadi śrīdhara , mudadi śrīdhara |

10

husiyanāḍi hòṭṭè hòrèva viṣayadalli rasikanèṃdu
husigè hākadiro ènna hṛṣīkeśane ||

11

biddu bhavadaneka januma baddanāgi kaluṣadiṃda
gèddu popa budhdhi torò padmanābhanè , padmanābhanè |

12

kāmakrodha biḍisi ninna nāma jihvèyòḻagè nuḍisu
śrīmahānubhāvanāda dāmodara ||

13

paṃkajākṣa nīnu ènna maṃkubuddhiyannu biḍisi
kiṃkaranna māḍikòḻḻo saṃkarṣaṇa , saṃkarṣaṇa |

14

esu janma baṃdarenu dāsanallavenu nānu
ghāsi māḍadiru innu vāsudevane ||

15

buddhi śūnyanāgi ènna baddhakāya kuhaka manava
tiddi hṛdaya śuddhi māḍo pradyumnane , pradyumnane |

16

janani janaka nīnèyèṃdu nènèvènayya dīnabaṃdhu
ènagè mukti pālisinnu aniruddhane ||

17

haruṣadiṃda ninna nāma smarisuvaṃtè māḍu nema
irisu caraṇadalli prema puruṣottama, puruṣottama
|

18

sādhusaṃga kòṭṭu ninna pādabhajanè ittu ènna
bhedamāḍi noḍadirò he adhokṣaja ||

19

cārucaraṇa tori ènagè pārugāṇisayya kònègè
bhāra hākiruvè ninagè nārasiṃhane , nārasiṃhane
|

20

saṃcitādi pāpagaḻanu kiṃcitāda pīḍègaḻanu
muṃcitāgi kaḻèdu pòrèyò svāmi acyuta ||

21

jñāna bhakuti kòṭṭu ninna dhyānadalli iṭṭu sadā
hīna buddhi biḍisò munna śrī janārdhana , śrī janārdhana |

22

japatapānuṣṭhānavilla dè kupitagāmiyāda ènna
kṛpèya māḍi kṣamisabeku he upeṃdrane ||

23

mòrèya iḍuvènayya ninagè śaradhiśayana śubhamatiya
iriso bhaktaròḻagè parama puruṣa śrīhare , puruṣa śrīhare |

24

huṭṭisalebeḍa innu huṭṭisidakè pālisinnu
iṣṭu mātra beḍikòṃbè śrī kṛṣṇane ||

25

satyavāda nāmagaḻanu nityadalli paṭhisuvavara
arthiyiṃda salahutiruva kartṛ keśava , keśava |

26

marèyadalè hariya nāma barèdu odi peḻdavarigè
karèdu mukti kòḍuva nèlèyādikeśava , nèlèyādikeśava || īśa ninna||
