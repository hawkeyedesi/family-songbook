Narasimhanembo Devanu

ಕನ್ನಡदेवनागरीEnglish (Roman)
Published full version (pallavi and 14 verses). Ajji sings a shorter selection beginning with verse 7, followed by verses 9–13; local oral wording differs. This is a comparison reading, not a verbatim transcription.

Narasimhanembo Devanu

01

nārasiṃhanèṃbò devanu nārasiṃhanèṃbò devanu|
naṃbidaṃtha nararigèlla varavakòḍuvanu

02

ādiyalli lakṣmīsahitadi malagiralu
baṃdarāga sanatkumāraru
āga dvārapālakaru taḍèyalāga kopadiṃda
mūru janmadalli nīvu asurarāgi puṭṭirèṃdaru

03

dvitīya garbhadalli janisida hiraṇyākṣa
hiraṇyakaśipurèṃba bhrātṛru
pṛthviyannu muḻugisida kāraṇādi śrīhariyu
tṛtīya rūpadiṃda khaḻana kòṃdu dharèyanuḻihidanu

04

anujanāda hiraṇyākṣana maraṇavannu
keḻi āga naḍèda tapassigè
hariya melè siṭṭiniṃda ugratapassannu māḍi
brahmaniṃda varagaḻannu paḍèdukòṃḍu baṃdanāga

05

iṃdraloka sūrè māḍida
mūru lokakè asura tanna bhayava torida
garbhiṇi kayāduvannu brahmaputra baṃdu āga
daityanāda hiraṇyakaśipuvigè òppisidanu

06

navamāsagaḻu tuṃbalu kayādu
āga putraratnavannu paḍèdaḻu
nāmakaraṇavannu māḍi viprarannu karèsi bega
daitya tanna sutagè prahlādanèṃdu karèsidanu

07

bālacaṃdranaṃtè hòḻèyutā irutiralu
aidu varṣa tuṃbitāgalè
gurugaḻannu karèsi bega sakala vidyè kalisirèṃdu
gurugaḻigè maganannu òppisida daitya tānu

08

oṃ namaḥ śivāya ènutali
asura tanna sutana barèdu toru ènalu
narahariya nāmavannu nagunagutalè barèyutiralu
èḍada tòḍèya melidda śiśuva baḍidu dharègè nūkidanu

09

sutta janara karèsi begadi
asura tanna sutana kòllisabekèṃdāga
aṭṭa aḍaviyòḻu viṣavaniṭṭu bhojanaṃgaḻa māḍi
hariya smaraṇè mātradiṃda bhuṃji tiḻida jaṭṭihāṃgè

10

aṃbudhiyòḻu magana malagisi
melè dòḍḍa bèṭṭavaniṭṭu banniro
hariya kṛpègè vaśanāda taraḻanèṃdu varuṇadeva
maraṇa illadahāṃgè māḍi manègè kòṭṭu kaḻuhidanu

11

bèṭṭadiṃda kaṭṭi uruḻisi asura
tanna paṭṭadānè kālali mèṭṭisi
hariva òlèya māḍi suṭṭu huliya boninalliṭṭu
yatnavilladè sutana kòllalu śaktanalladè podanaṃtè

12

ninna deva idda èḍèyanu toru
ènuta pitanu tanna sutana koralu
ènna deva illadaṃta èḍègaḻuṃṭè lokadalli
kaṃbadallū iruvanèṃdu kaiya mugidu tordanāgè

13

varakaṃbavannu òḍèyalu narahariyu ugrakopavannu tāḻida
kaṭakaṭèṃba dhvaniya māḍi nakhagaḻiṃda
piḍidu òtti karuḻa bagèdu
mālè hāki kaṃda bhaktanappikòṃḍa

14

aṃtarikṣadali amararu noḍi āga
puṣpavṛṣṭiyannè karèdaru
ajana paḍèda devi baṃdu tòḍèya melè kuḻitiralu
amarapatiyarèllara noḍi aṃjabeḍèṃdabhayavitta

15

lakṣmīnārasiṃha caritèya
udayakāla paṭhisuvaṃta nararigèlla putrasaṃtānaṃgaḻittu
mattè beḍidahāṃgè kòṭṭu
bhaktavatsala muktikòḍuva puraṃdaraviṭhalarāya
