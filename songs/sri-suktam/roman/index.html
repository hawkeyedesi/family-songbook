The hymn to Mahalakshmi, with the book’s closing prayers.

Family reading

01

hiraṇyavarṇāṃ hariṇīṃ suvarṇarajatasrajām |
caṃdrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ lakṣmīṃ jātavedo ma āvaha ||

02

tāṃ ma āvaha jātavedo lakṣmīmanapagāminīm |
yasyāṃ hiraṇyaṃ viṃdeyaṃ gāmaśvaṃ puruṣānaham ||

03

aśvapūrvāṃ rathamadhyāṃ hastinādaprabodhinīm |
śriyaṃ devīmupahvaye śrīrmā devī juṣatām ||

04

kāṃ so'smitāṃ hiraṇyaprākārāmārdrāṃ jvalaṃtīṃ tṛptāṃ tarpayaṃtīm |
padme sthitāṃ padmavarṇāṃ tāmihopahvaye śriyam ||

05

caṃdrāṃ prabhāsāṃ yaśasā jvalaṃtīṃ śriyaṃ loke devajuṣṭāmudārām |
tāṃ padminīmīṃ śaraṇamahaṃ prapadye'lakṣmīrme naśyatāṃ tvāṃ vṛṇe ||

06

ādityavarṇe tapaso'dhijāto vanaspatistava vṛkṣo'tha bilvaḥ |
tasya phalāni tapasā nudaṃtu māyāṃtarāyāśca bāhyā alakṣmīḥ ||

07

upaitu māṃ devasakhaḥ kīrtiśca maṇinā saha |
prādurbhūto'smi rāṣṭre'smin kīrtimṛddhiṃ dadātu me ||

08

kṣutpipāsāmalāṃ jyeṣṭhāmalakṣmīṃ nāśayāmyaham |
abhūtimasamṛddhiṃ ca sarvāṃ nirṇuda me gṛhāt ||

09

gaṃdhadvārāṃ durādharṣāṃ nityapuṣṭāṃ karīṣiṇīm |
īśvarīṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tāmihopahvaye śriyam ||

10

manasaḥ kāmamākūtiṃ vācaḥ satyamaśīmahi |
paśūnāṃ rūpamannasya mayi śrīḥ śrayatāṃ yaśaḥ ||

11

kardamena prajābhūtā mayi saṃbhava kardama |
śriyaṃ vāsaya me kule mātaraṃ padmamālinīm ||

12

āpaḥ sṛjaṃtu snigdhāni ciklīta vasa me gṛhe |
ni ca devīṃ mātaraṃ śriyaṃ vāsaya me kule ||

13

ārdrāṃ puṣkariṇīṃ puṣṭiṃ suvarṇāṃ hemamālinīm |
sūryāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ lakṣmīṃ jātavedo ma āvaha ||

14

ārdrāṃ yaḥ kariṇīṃ yaṣṭiṃ piṃgalāṃ padmamālinīm |
caṃdrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ lakṣmīṃ jātavedo ma āvaha ||

15

tāṃ ma āvaha jātavedo lakṣmīmanapagāminīm |
yasyāṃ hiraṇyaṃ prabhūtaṃ gāvo dāsyo'śvān viṃdeyaṃ puruṣānaham ||

16

mahādevyai ca vidmahe viṣṇupatnyai ca dhīmahi |
tanno lakṣmīḥ pracodayāt ||

17

abhirgīrbhiryadato na ūnamāpyāyaya harivo vardhamānaḥ |
yadā stotṛbhyo mahi gotrā rujāsi bhūyiṣṭhabhājo adha te syāma ||
brahma prāvādiṣma tanno mā hāsīt |
oṃ śāṃtiḥ śāṃtiḥ śāṃtiḥ ||

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below.
