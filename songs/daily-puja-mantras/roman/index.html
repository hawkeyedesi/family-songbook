Daily Puja Mantras

Seven mantra passages from the family’s puja book.

yo vedādau

01

yo vedādau svaraḥ prokto vedāṃte ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
tasya prakṛtilīnasya yaḥ paraḥ sa maheśvaraḥ ||

gaurīrmimāya

02

gaurīrmimāya salilāni takṣatyekapadī dvipadī sā catuṣpadī |
aṣṭāpadī navapadī babhūvuṣī sahasrākṣarā parame vyoman ||

gaṇānāṃ tvā

03

gaṇānāṃ tvā gaṇapatiṃ havāmahe kaviṃ kavīnāmupamaśravastamam |
jyeṣṭharājaṃ brahmaṇāṃ brahmaṇaspata ā naḥ śṛṇvannūtibhiḥ sīda sādanam ||

tadviṣṇoḥ

04

tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padaṃ sadā paśyaṃti sūrayaḥ |
divīva cakṣurātatam ||
tadviprāso vipanyavo jāgṛvāṃsaḥ samiṃdhate |
viṣṇoryatparamaṃ padam ||

ā kṛṣṇena

05

ā kṛṣṇena rajasā vartamāno niveśayannamṛtaṃ martyaṃ ca |
hiraṇyayena savitā rathenādevo yāti bhuvanāni paśyan ||

rājādhirājāya

06

rājādhirājāya prasahyasāhine namo vayaṃ vaiśravaṇāya kurmahe |
sa me kāmān kāmakāmāya mahyaṃ kāmeśvaro vaiśravaṇo dadātu |
kuberāya vaiśravaṇāya mahārājāya namaḥ ||

paryāptyā

07

paryāptyā anaṃtarāyāya sarvastomo'tirātra uttamamaharbhavati |
sarvasyāptyai sarvasya jityai sarvameva tenāpnoti sarvaṃ jayati ||

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below. These are the same seven selected mantra passages from the family page, not an expanded daily-puja manual. Vedic accent marks remain in the scan rather than being reconstructed.
