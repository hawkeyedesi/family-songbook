Lakshmi Nrisimha Karavalamba

A prayer to Lakshmi Nrisimha for a helping hand.

Choose script / ಲಿಪಿ ಆಯ್ಕೆ
ಕನ್ನಡसंस्कृतम्English (Roman)
Jump to a section:Family reading

Family reading

01

śrīmatpayonidhiniketana cakrapāṇe bhogīṃdrabhogamaṇirājita puṇyamūrte |
yogīśa śāśvata śaraṇya bhavābdhipota lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

02

brahmeṃdrarudramarudarkakirīṭakoṭi saṃghaṭṭitāṃghrikamalāmalakāṃtikāṃta |
lakṣmīlasatkucasaroruharājahaṃsa lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

03

saṃsāradāvadahanākarabhīkaroru-jvālāvalībhiratidagdhatanūruhasya |
tvatpādapadmasarasīruhamāgatasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

04

saṃsārajālapatitasya jagannivāsa sarveṃdriyārtha baḍiśāgra jhaṣopamasya |
protkaṃpita pracuratāluka mastakasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

05

saṃsārakūpamatighoramagādhamūlaṃ saṃprāpya duḥkhaśatasarpasamākulasya |
dīnasya deva kṛpayā padamāgatasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

06

saṃsārabhīkarakarīṃdrakarābhighāta niṣpīḍyamānavapuṣaḥ sakalārtināśa |
prāṇaprayāṇabhavabhītisamākulasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

07

saṃsārasarpaviṣadigdhamahogratīvra daṃṣṭrāgrakoṭiparidaṣṭavinaṣṭamūrteḥ |
nāgārivāhana sudhābdhinivāsa śaure lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

08

saṃsāravṛkṣamaghabījamanaṃtakarma-śākhāyutaṃ karaṇapatramanaṃgapuṣpam |
āruhya duḥkhaphalitaṃ patato dayālo lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

09

saṃsārasāgaraviśālakarālakāla nakragrahagrasitanigrahavigrahasya |
vyagrasya rāganicayorminipīḍitasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

10

saṃsārasāgaranimajjanamuhyamānaṃ dīnaṃ vilokaya vibho karuṇānidhe mām |
prahlādakhedaparihāraparāvatāra lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

11

saṃsāraghoragahane carato murāre mārograbhīkaramṛgapracurārditasya |
ārtasya matsaranidāghasuduḥkhitasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

12

baddhvā gale yamabhaṭā bahu tarjayaṃtaḥ karṣaṃti yatra bhavapāśaśatairyutaṃ mām |
ekākinaṃ paravaśaṃ cakitaṃ dayālo lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

13

lakṣmīpate kamalanābha sureśa viṣṇo yajñeśa yajña madhusūdana viśvarūpa |
brahmaṇya keśava janārdana vāsudeva lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

14

ekena cakramapareṇa kareṇa śaṃkha-manyena siṃdhutanayāmavalaṃbya tiṣṭhan |
vāmetareṇa varadābhayapadmacihnaṃ lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

15

aṃdhasya me hṛtavivekamahādhanasya corairmahābalibhiriṃdriyanāmadheyaiḥ |
mohāṃdhakārakuhare vinipātitasya lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

16

prahlādanāradaparāśarapuṃḍarīka-vyāsādibhāgavatapuṃgavahṛnnivāsa |
bhaktānuraktaparipālanapārijāta lakṣmīnṛsiṃha mama dehi karāvalaṃbam

17

lakṣmīnṛsiṃhacaraṇābjamadhuvratena stotraṃ kṛtaṃ śubhakaraṃ bhuvi śaṃkareṇa |
ye tatpaṭhaṃti manujā haribhaktiyuktā-ste yāṃti tatpadasarojamakhaṃḍarūpam

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below.
