Pallavi

pallavi
bhāgyadā lakṣmī bārammā
nammamma nī sau-bhāgyadā lakṣmī bārammā ॥

Charanam 1

charaṇaṃ 1
hejjeye mēlē hejjeya nikkuta
gejje kālgaḻā dhvaniya tōruta (māḍuta)
sajjana sādhū pūjeya vēḻege
majjigeyoḻagina beṇṇeyante ॥

(bhāgyadā)

Charanam 2

charaṇaṃ 2
kanakāvṛṣṭiya kareyuta bārē
manakāmaneyā siddhiya tōre ।
dinakarakōṭī tējadi hoḻeyuva
janakarāyanā kumāri bēga ॥

(bhāgyadā)

Charanam 3

charaṇaṃ 3
attittalagade bhaktara maneyali
nitya mahōtsava nitya sumaṅgala ।
satyava tōruva sādhu sajjanara
chittadi hoḻeyuva putthaḻi bombe ॥

(bhāgyadā)

Charanam 4

charaṇaṃ 4
saṅkhyē illada bhāgyava koṭṭu
kaṅkaṇa kaiyā tiruvuta bāre ।
kuṅkumāṅkite paṅkaja lōchane
vēṅkaṭa ramaṇana biṅkadarāṇī ॥

(bhāgyadā)

Charanam 5

charaṇaṃ 5
sakkare tuppada kāluveharisi
śukravāradā pūjeya vēḻege ।
akkareyuḻḻa aḻagiri raṅganana
chokka purandara viṭhṭhalana rāṇī ॥

(bhāgyadā)

Sources & text notes

One published edition in three scripts. Roman text is for pronunciation, not an English translation. Textual variants are retained. Purandara Dasa’s Kannada composition: Devanagari also preserves the Kannada words, not a Sanskrit translation. Pallavi and all five charanams are included. This is distinct from Amma Baaramma. Three source spelling errors are corrected against the Kannada comparison: attittalagade, sakkare and akkare; the same corrections are applied in all scripts.
