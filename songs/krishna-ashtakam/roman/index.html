Sri Krishna Ashtakam

Shankaracharya’s hymn beginning “Shriyaashlishto Vishnuh”.

Choose script / ಲಿಪಿ ಆಯ್ಕೆ

Family reading

01

śriyāśliṣṭo viṣṇuḥ sthiracaragururvedaviṣayo
dhiyāṃ sākṣī śuddho harirasurahaṃtābjanayanaḥ |
gadī śaṃkhī cakrī vimalavanamālī sthiraruciḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

02

yataḥ sarvaṃ jātaṃ viyadanilamukhyaṃ jagadidaṃ
sthitau niḥśeṣaṃ yo'vati nijasukhāṃśena madhuhā |
laye sarvaṃ svasminharati kalayā yastu sa vibhuḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

03

asūnāyamyādau yamaniyamamukhyaiḥ sukaraṇai-
rniruddhyedaṃ cittaṃ hṛdi vilayamānīya sakalam |
yamīḍyaṃ paśyaṃti pravaramatayo māyinamasau
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

04

pṛthivyāṃ tiṣṭhanyo yamayati mahīṃ veda na dharā
yamityādau vedo vadati jagatāmīśamamalam |
niyaṃtāraṃ dhyeyaṃ munisuranṛṇāṃ mokṣadamasau
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

05

maheṃdrādirdevo jayati ditijānyasya balato
na kasya svātaṃtryaṃ kvacidapi kṛtau yatkṛtimṛte |
balārātergarvaṃ pariharati yo'sau vijayinaḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

06

vinā yasya dhyānaṃ vrajati paśutāṃ sūkaramukhāṃ
vinā yasya jñānaṃ janimṛtibhayaṃ yāti janatā |
vinā yasya smṛtyā kṛmiśatajaniṃ yāti sa vibhuḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

07

narātaṃkoṭṭaṃkaḥ śaraṇaśaraṇo bhrāṃtiharaṇo
ghanaśyāmo vāmo vrajaśiśuvayasyo'rjunasakhaḥ |
svayaṃbhūrbhūtānāṃ janaka ucitācārasukhadaḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

08

yathā dharmaglānirbhavati jagatāṃ kṣobhakaraṇī
tadā lokasvāmī prakaṭitavapuḥ setudhṛdajaḥ |
satāṃ dhātā svaccho nigamagaṇagīto vrajapatiḥ
śaraṇyo lokeśo mama bhavatu kṛṣṇo'kṣiviṣayaḥ

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below.
