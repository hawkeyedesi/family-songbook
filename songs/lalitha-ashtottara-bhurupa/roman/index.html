dhyānam

sindūrāruṇavigrahāṃ trinayanāṃ māṇikyamaulisphurat-
tārānāyakaśekharāṃ smitamamukhīmāpīnavakṣoruhām |
pāṇibhyāmatipūrṇaratnacaṣakaṃ raktotpalaṃ vibhratīṃ
saumyāṃ ratnaghaṭastharaktacaraṇāṃ dhyāyet parāmambikām ||
aruṇāṃ karuṇātaraṅgitākṣīṃ dhṛtapāśāṅkuśapuṣpabāṇacāpām |
aṇimādibhirāvṛtāṃ mayūkhairahamityeva vibhāvaye bhavānīm ||
dhyāyet padmāsanasthāṃ vikasitavadanāṃ padmapatrāyatākṣīm
hemābhāṃ pītavastrāṃ karakalitalasaddhemapadmāṃ varāṅgīm |
sarvālaṅkāra-yuktāṃ satatamabhayadāṃ bhaktanamrāṃ bhavānīm
śrīvidyāṃ śāntamūrtiṃ sakalasuranutāṃ sarvasampatpradātrīm ||

Sources & text notes

The published Sanskrit Bhurupa version matches the recording’s distinctive name sequence, including ghranarūpa, gandharūpa and gandhagrahanakarinī. All 108 names, the source’s three dhyana verses and closing are included. Kannada and Roman are deterministic, round-trip-checked script renderings, not translations or ASR guesses. Discretionary line-break hyphens are joined. The separately supplied Rajatachala version remains a separate entry.
