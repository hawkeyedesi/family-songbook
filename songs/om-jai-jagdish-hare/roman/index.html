Sources & text notes

Family-book reading and song order. Kannada/Devanagari are script renderings; Roman is pronunciation text, not an English meaning translation. Return to the opening chorus after each stanza, as marked in the book. This is a Hindi arati, not a Sanskrit translation. The supplied Roman lines resolve the damaged Devanagari printing; the family Roman singing expansions are retained.
