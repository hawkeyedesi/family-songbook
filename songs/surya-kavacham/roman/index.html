Surya Kavacham

Yajnavalkya’s seven-verse hymn to Surya.

Family reading

01

śṛṇuṣva muniśārdūla sūryasya kavacaṃ śubham |
śarīrārogyadaṃ divyaṃ sarvasaubhāgyadāyakam

02

dedīpyamānamukuṭaṃ sphuranmakarakuṃḍalam |
dhyātvā sahasrakiraṇaṃ stotrametadudīrayet

03

śiro me bhāskaraḥ pātu lalāṭaṃ me'mitadyutiḥ |
netre dinamaṇiḥ pātu śravaṇe vāsareśvaraḥ

04

ghrāṇaṃ gharmaghṛṇiḥ pātu vadanaṃ vedavāhanaḥ |
jihvāṃ me mānadaḥ pātu kaṃṭhaṃ me suravaṃditaḥ

05

skaṃdhau prabhākaraḥ pātu vakṣaḥ pātu janapriyaḥ |
pātu pādau dvādaśātmā sarvāṃgaṃ sakaleśvaraḥ

06

sūryarakṣātmakaṃ stotraṃ likhitvā bhūrjapatrake |
dadhāti yaḥ kare tasya vaśagāḥ sarvasiddhayaḥ

07

susnāto yo japet samyagyo'dhīte svasthamānasaḥ |
sa rogamukto dīrghāyuḥ sukhaṃ puṣṭiṃ ca viṃdati

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below.
