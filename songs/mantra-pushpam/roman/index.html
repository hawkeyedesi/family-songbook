Svara & pronunciation key
  • a̠ / a̱Anudātta — low pitch
  • a̍Svarita — falling pitch
  • a̎Long/double svarita — follow the source tradition’s timing

These are pitch marks, not stress accents. Unmarked syllables are not automatically all udātta: the notation also leaves pracaya unmarked. ā, ī, ū mark long vowels, not pitch; e/o are long Sanskrit vowels even when a source prints them without a macron. ś/ṣ/s are distinct; ṭ/ḍ/ṇ are retroflex. ṃ/ṁ marks anusvāra; ḥ (sometimes : in the source) marks visarga. Keep the source’s nasal and sandhi notation; exact recitation follows the taught śākhā.

Sources & text notes

One published edition in three scripts. Roman text is for pronunciation, not an English translation. Textual variants are retained. Includes the source’s opening peace prayer and closing mantras. Other puja compilations may add Narayana Suktam and additional Gayatris; these are not silently blended into this edition.
