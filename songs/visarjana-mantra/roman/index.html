Svara & pronunciation key
  • a̠ / a̱Anudātta — low pitch
  • a̍Svarita — falling pitch
  • a̎Long/double svarita — follow the source tradition’s timing

These are pitch marks, not stress accents. Unmarked syllables are not automatically all udātta: the notation also leaves pracaya unmarked. ā, ī, ū mark long vowels, not pitch; e/o are long Sanskrit vowels even when a source prints them without a macron. ś/ṣ/s are distinct; ṭ/ḍ/ṇ are retroflex. ṃ/ṁ marks anusvāra; ḥ (sometimes : in the source) marks visarga. Keep the source’s nasal and sandhi notation; exact recitation follows the taught śākhā.

Sources & text notes

Added from the family-book page (xviii). The two Vedic passages are Rigveda 1.40.1 and 8.72.11; their text and pitch marks come from the cited Sanskrit Documents edition, not from guesses about the unaccented photo. The photo appears to print “ಅಭ್ಯಾರಮಿದ ದ್ಯೋ”; the reading above follows the published “अभ्यारमिदद्रयो”. Printed spacing is normalized in the reading edition. The closing “Pūjāvasāne gacchatvaṃ punarāgamanāya ca” is retained from the family book without invented accents. The original Kannada transcription and cropped page remain below. The book instructs: after this mantra, gently move the kalasha/image slightly to the right and set the flowers aside; it calls this the udvāsana procedure. This is not yet checked against a Sringeri edition. It is a separate closing reading, not a newly timed cue in the 1983 recording.
