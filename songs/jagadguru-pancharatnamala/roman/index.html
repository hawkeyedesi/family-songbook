śrījagadgurupañcaratnamālāstotram

candramaulipūjakaṃ samastapāpanāśakaṃ
bhaktacittavaśakaṃ sadātmaniṣṭhamānasam ।
bhasmanā vibhūṣitaṃ karāttadaṇḍaśobhitaṃ
śṛṅgaśailabhūṣaṇaṃ namāmi taṃ jagadgurum ॥ 1 ॥

candracūḍasadguroḥ karābjasambhavaṃ vibhuṃ
jñānamuktidāyinaṃ natottamākṛtiṃ prabhum ।
mantraśāstravāridhiṃ kṣamānidhiṃ taponidhiṃ
śṛṅgaśailabhūṣaṇaṃ namāmi taṃ jagadgurum ॥ 2 ॥

vedatattvabodhakaṃ svadharmamārgadarśakaṃ
bhaktavatsalaṃ śubhaṃ sutīkṣṇabuddhidāyakam ।
hāsapūrvabhāṣiṇaṃ smitānanaṃ parātparaṃ
śṛṅgaśailabhūṣaṇaṃ namāmi taṃ jagadgurum ॥ 3 ॥

dīnalokarakṣakaṃ hi pāpalokadurlabhaṃ
ādareṇa śiṣyakoṭisevitāṅghripaṅkajam ।
bhaktatāpahāriṇaṃ yatīśvaraṃ maheśvaraṃ
śṛṅgaśailabhūṣaṇaṃ namāmi taṃ jagadgurum ॥ 4 ॥

candraśekharākhyasadguroḥ padābjasevakaṃ
mohanāśakaṃ śivaṃ gaṇeśaśāradārcakam ।
dvaitabhītivāraṇaṃ dayānidhiṃ gurorguruṃ
śṛṅgaśailabhūṣaṇaṃ namāmi taṃ jagadgurum ॥ 5 ॥

pañcakaṃ sadgurornityaṃ bhaktyā prapaṭhatāṃ nṛṇām ।
āyurārogyamaiśvaryaṃ bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ॥

Sources & text notes

Family-book reading and song order. Kannada/Devanagari are script renderings; Roman is pronunciation text, not an English meaning translation. The family page’s five verses and closing verse are retained. Roman spellings are rendered into Sanskrit orthography; Sharadacakam is read as Sharadarcakam (worshipper of Sharada). The original page remains available for comparison.
