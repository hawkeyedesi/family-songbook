Durga Suktam

The Vedic hymn of refuge in Durga.

Family reading

01

oṃ jātavedase sunavāma somamarātīyato nidahāti vedaḥ |
sa naḥ parṣadati durgāṇi viśvā nāveva siṃdhuṃ duritātyagniḥ ||

02

tāmagnivarṇāṃ tapasā jvalaṃtīṃ vairocanīṃ karmaphaleṣu juṣṭām |
durgāṃ devīṃ śaraṇamahaṃ prapadye sutarasi tarase namaḥ ||

03

agne tvaṃ pārayā navyo asmānsvastibhirati durgāṇi viśvā |
pūśca pṛthvī bahulā na urvī bhavā tokāya tanayāya śaṃyoḥ ||

04

viśvāni no durgahā jātavedaḥ siṃdhuṃ na nāvā duritātiparṣi |
agne atrivanmanasā gṛṇāno'smākaṃ bodhyavitā tanūnām ||

05

pṛtanājitaṃ sahamānamugramagniṃ huvema paramātsadhasthāt |
sa naḥ parṣadati durgāṇi viśvā kṣāmaddevo ati duritātyagniḥ ||

06

pratnoṣi kamīḍyo adhvareṣu sanācca hotā navyaśca satsi |
svāṃ cāgne tanuvaṃ piprayasvāsmabhyaṃ ca saubhagamāyajasva ||

07

gobhirjuṣṭamayujo niṣiktaṃ taveṃdra viṣṇoranusaṃcarema |
nākasya pṛṣṭhamabhi saṃvasāno vaiṣṇavīṃ loka iha mādayaṃtām ||

08

oṃ kātyāyanāya vidmahe kanyakumāri dhīmahi |
tanno durgiḥ pracodayāt ||

09

oṃ śāṃtiḥ śāṃtiḥ śāṃtiḥ ||

Sources & text notes

The family Kannada reading is preserved exactly. Devanagari and Roman are script renderings of that same reading, not translations or substituted editions. Standard Sanskrit letter forms are used where regional Kannada spelling differs. Original scans and comparison sources remain below.
