Amma Baaramma

A devotional invitation to the Divine Mother.

Choose script / ಲಿಪಿ ಆಯ್ಕೆ
ಕನ್ನಡदेवनागरीEnglish (Roman)
Jump to a section:ChorusVerses

Chorus

Amma baaramma namma Tayi baaramma
Taayi baaramma namma Deviye baaramma

Verses

01

Moonu loka dodeya Sivanaa raaniye baaramma
Ninnaya Gejjeya Nada galu-galu-yennuta Tayi baaramma

02

Gajamukha jananiye dheera gambhirale, Gayatri deviye baaramma
Amba bhavaniye Ganga bhavaniye, Tulasiya bhavaniye Baaramma

03

Shankara stapithe Sri Chakra poojithe, Sharada Deviye baaramma
Sringeri-yalli nagu-nagutiruvaa Bharati Deviye baaramma

04

Khadga dhaarini trishula paanee, Chamundeshwari baaramma
Rajaadhi-rajaarinda poojisalpaduvantha, Rajarajeshwari baaramma

05

Japavanaa ariyenu Tapavanaa ariyenu, Moodala karunisu baaramma
Moojaga vandite murahara nollabhe, Mookambike mate baaramma

06

Kanchi Kamakshi, Madhurai Meenakshi, Kasi Vishalakshi baaramma
Kamitha phalagata needuvantaa karunaa sagari baaramma

07

Sri Jayalakshmi Jaya Jaya Laksmi, Prasanna Laksmiye baaramma
Sravana masadi poojisalpaduvantha, Varamaha-Laksmiye baaramma

08

Sri Jaya Gowri, Sirivara Gowri, Mangala Gowriye baaramma
Bhadrapada maasadi poojisalpaduvantha Swarna Gowriye baaramma

09

Navrathri Devi moola Saraswathi, Ashtami Durgiye baaramma
Dasamiya dinadolu, bijayaangaiyalu vijaya Laksmiye baaramma

10

Adi Shakti Akhilandeshwari, Annapoorneshwari baaramma
Ashrita janarige abhaya needalu, Santhoshi Mateye baaramma

Sources & text notes

The original Roman text is preserved verbatim. Kannada and Devanagari are phonetic script renderings of the same Kannada song, not Sanskrit translations. Ambiguous book spellings such as Moonu, Moodala and phalagata are retained phonetically, not silently corrected; their Kannada readings still need family confirmation. The source photo remains available.
